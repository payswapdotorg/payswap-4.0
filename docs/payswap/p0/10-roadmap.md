## Approved P3–P9 product direction (revised 2026-11-25)

P3–P9 deliver PaySwap as one payment-only product with two complementary user-facing capabilities: (1) a unified, primary interface for connected PSP accounts and the payment operations those providers actually support; and (2) intent-driven money-movement route and opportunity discovery. Users can connect payment-provider accounts, inspect evidence-backed payment data, and perform supported payment-related read/write operations through PaySwap rather than switching between provider dashboards for routine payment work. They can also state the financial outcome they want, and PaySwap evaluates the sender's and recipient's verified payment capabilities before discovering direct, composed and multi-party routes. PaySwap does not discover or integrate general-purpose, non-payment service providers; a third party may participate only as a financial party contributing a verified payment capability and explicitly accepting its role in a route. ZCode's existing runtime and agents may assist with payment reasoning, but there is no customer-facing general-purpose agent marketplace or second agent runtime. There is one provider capability authority, authorization authority, execution authority and financial evidence ledger. PaySwap remains non-custodial: it does not hold customer funds, issue stored value, or gain authority over another participant's account. The binding architecture is [12-agent-payment-harness-architecture.md](12-agent-payment-harness-architecture.md), constrained by [11-architecture-addendum.md](11-architecture-addendum.md). This product clarification supersedes the broader agent-discovery and general business-mission framing in the 2026-11-15 direction; it does not change P0–P3 implementation history or evidence.

TL2's Linux work is paused until the Windows product has completed P9; preserve existing branches, test results and evidence, but do not continue Linux implementation or P2-Linux acceptance during P1–P9.

- Keep already-merged cross-platform code and existing CI regression jobs. Do not remove or weaken cross-platform tests, shared security laws, or regression gates; CI coverage does not substitute for native Windows acceptance.
- The post-P9 queue includes P2-Linux's remaining native acceptance, Darwin peer-verification helper production packaging (ADR-15), the H1–H6 Windows custodian hardening backlog, and other non-blocking platform-hardening items. Keep them in their existing evidence/backlog records; do not silently drop or promote them into P1–P9 scope.

If a newly confirmed vulnerability makes an enabled Windows path unsafe, isolate/refuse that path and address the immediate risk before continuing; it is not deferred as routine hardening.
- P9's Windows product, corridor/legal and operational acceptance criteria remain in force. Only the explicitly deferred non-MVP platform work and non-blocking hardening backlog move beyond P9. The separately operator-controlled live-money gate remains unchanged.
